One warning label (152 characters, limit 160) for every human being: funny first, plausible second. Top five split the $5 pot equally.

## Why this line

Real label wording (WARNING, do not shake, do not ask) applied to a recognisable human state, with the escalation in the last clause — crying at dog videos without warning. The label is plausible enough to picture printed on a person.
